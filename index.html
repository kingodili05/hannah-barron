The Life I Live

What I Do

Hand-grabbing catfish out of submerged holes. Whitetail through Alabama timber. Rivers, trotlines, jugs. One world, three obsessions.

01 / Noodling
Noodling
Reaching bare-handed into underwater holes for hundred-pound flatheads. Dangerous and addictive.
02 / Hunting
Hunting
Bow and rifle. Whitetail, turkey, hog — Alabama timber, patience, and respect for the land.
03 / Fishing
Fishing
Rod, jug, trotline, limb line. River bottoms before sunrise. Whatever puts fish in the boat.
