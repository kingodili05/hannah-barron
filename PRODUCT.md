# Hannah Barron Outdoors — Product Brief

## Register
**Brand.** Marketing surface for an outdoor creator. Design IS the product. Visitor's first-pass impression must do the heavy lifting; conversion (merch, brand deals, fan signup) follows from belief in the persona.

## Audience
- **Core fans** — outdoor enthusiasts in the Southeast US, ages 18–45, mostly mobile, came in from YouTube/IG/TikTok. They want behind-the-scenes access and merch that looks earned, not Etsy.
- **Brand partners** — outdoor-industry marketers (Realtree, Bass Pro, optic/gun/apparel brands). They want a media kit that signals reach + credibility.
- **Press / media** — booking podcasts, interviews, features. Want a tight bio + contact path.

## Product purpose
A single creator hub that:
1. Sells merch (Stripe checkout).
2. Drives social platform follows.
3. Houses brand-partnership pitch surface (contact + press).
4. Runs sweepstakes ("any 2025 vehicle of your choice") with a private winner-token flow.

## Tone / brand voice
Three words, picked deliberately: **kinetic, mud-honest, woods-native**.
- Kinetic: she does things — grabs catfish bare-handed, takes whitetail at dawn. Site should feel like motion, not catalog.
- Mud-honest: no polish for polish's sake. Bone surfaces have grit, photos aren't over-retouched.
- Woods-native: Alabama-rooted. Not "outdoor lifestyle" stock photography — actual place.

## Anti-references (what we will NOT look like)
- **Saturated SaaS-creator template:** sans-serif on cream, pastel gradient hero, icon-card grid, "Join the community" bullets. (The current site lives partly here.)
- **Editorial-magazine register:** display serif + italic drop caps + ruled separators. Wrong genre.
- **Camo-everywhere "redneck" register:** Realtree-pattern backgrounds, fake distressed type. Costume.
- **Yetis-and-tumblers premium-outdoor reflex:** navy + cream + cursive script. Too coastal-brewery.

## Direct references (the lane we're in)
Sitka Gear, First Lite, KUIU. Modern athletic / technical hunting brand. Photo-led, tactical palette, condensed industrial type, tight grids. Heritage of Filson without the lumberjack costume.

## Strategic principles
- **Photo is content; UI is frame.** Surfaces exist to serve hannah.jpg, hannah2.jpg, hannah3.jpg, hannah4.jpg — not compete with them.
- **No emoji as primary affordance.** Current site uses 🎣 as the logo and 🐟/🦌 as section icons. Replace with inline SVG marks and photography.
- **Dark hero, bone interior.** A hunter wakes at 5am in dim light; the site greets her dark. Product detail and form work happens on bone — lighter for legibility and product confidence.
- **One accent. One.** Rust orange (not yellow, not safety-blaze). Used at <15% surface area, almost always on action.
- **Conversion is implicit.** Don't pile CTAs. One clear ask per screen.
